Learning the basics

Progressive overload

Doing a little more over time so your muscles keep having a reason to grow. Once you hit 10 reps on every working set with good form, add a small amount of weight next session and build back up from 6.

Hypertrophy

The scientific word for muscle growth. Muscles grow when you train them close to failure, recover properly, and eat enough protein. Around 1.6 g of protein per kg of bodyweight a day is a common target.

Calorie deficit

Eating fewer calories than you burn, so your body uses stored energy, mostly fat, to make up the difference. A moderate deficit of about 300–500 calories a day is easier to stick to and helps you keep muscle.

Cutting

A phase where you eat in a calorie deficit to lose fat while keeping your muscle. Keep lifting heavy and keep protein high. Losing around 0.5–1% of your bodyweight a week is a sensible pace.

Bulking

A phase where you eat slightly more than you burn to give your body extra material to build muscle. A small surplus of about 200–300 calories a day limits how much fat you gain alongside the muscle.
